Criterion A — Inquiring & Analysing

One worked exemplar for each community problem: design need, research plan, existing products and design brief.

Choose your community problem

Each exemplar follows the school Criterion A booklet (A1 to A4 plus Appendix A). Use it to see the standard, then write your own about a problem you have investigated.

What each exemplar contains

  • A1 Design need: a continuous essay with statistics, a key-figures graphic and a mind map.
  • A2 Research plan: six questions (factual, conceptual, debatable) with priority, primary or secondary type and evidence.
  • A3 Existing products: three different products with images, ACCESS FM analysis, pros and cons.
  • A4 Design brief: a 350 to 600 word essay that restates the need, uses the research and states the design intent.
  • Appendix A: MLA 9 sources, notes, and blank primary research instruments.
